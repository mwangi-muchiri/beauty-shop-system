Our story

A warm welcome, expert hands, and room to be yourself.

DUN GLOW began with a simple belief: beauty care should leave you feeling more like yourself, never less. We created a welcoming beauty shop where expert technique meets genuine conversation and thoughtful care.

Today, our team brings together talented hair stylists, makeup artists, nail professionals, and skincare specialists. We listen first, share honest advice, and make each visit feel personal—whether you are stopping by for a quick refresh or preparing for a big celebration.
