About us

Experienced, strategic, and client focused legal advocacy

Muchiri Munene & Co. Advocates is a full-service law firm committed to delivering clear advice, strong representation, and practical legal solutions for individuals, businesses, and public institutions.

We are a results-driven law firm based in Westlands, Nairobi, serving a diverse clientele across corporate, commercial, family, property, and dispute resolution matters.
